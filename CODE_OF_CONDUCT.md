# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Martech Verify is an evidence-first project. Discussion should make the software safer,
clearer and more useful while treating every participant with respect.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and results rather than attacking people;
- giving specific, reproducible and constructive feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty, mistakes and the limits of available evidence;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels and communication styles;
- protecting customer, employee and contributor privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, impersonation, or publishing another person's private
information. Never post production exports, credentials, customer records, personal data,
or sensitive vulnerability details to make a point or reproduce a bug.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other
spaces where someone is representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when behavior violates
this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when safe, and
focused on protecting the community.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's report or block controls on the relevant content. Follow GitHub's
[abuse-reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for harassment, doxxing, threats, or other platform abuse.
Do not repeat private information in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities belong in
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/m7mdwb/martech-verify/security/advisories/new),
following [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Good-faith reports will not be penalized. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern
is itself a violation of this policy.
